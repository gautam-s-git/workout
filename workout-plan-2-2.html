🎯 DAILY WAIST FIX MOVES

Do these every single day — even on Sunday rest day. Just 10–15 minutes and it makes a huge difference in correcting your right-side waist drop.

📐 Right Side Plank
Lie on right side, lift hips off floor. Hold. Directly strengthens the right waist muscle (quadratus lumborum) pulling your waist down.
3 × 30–45 SEC DAILY
🙆 Standing Side Lean Stretch
Raise left arm overhead, slowly lean right. Feel the deep stretch on your right waist. Hold and breathe. Releases the tight muscles causing the drop.
3 × 40 SEC EACH SIDE
🦋 Piriformis Stretch
Lie on back, cross right ankle over left knee, pull both legs toward chest. Releases the right hip which affects pelvic tilt and waist level.
3 × 30 SEC RIGHT SIDE PRIORITY
🏔️ Hip Flexor Lunge Stretch
Kneel on right knee, push hips forward gently. Tight right hip flexor is a key cause of pelvic tilt. Hold and breathe deeply throughout.
3 × 40 SEC EACH SIDE
🌉 Glute Bridge
Lie on back, feet flat, lift hips until body is straight. Focus on lifting both sides equally. Builds even glute strength to stabilize pelvis.
3 × 15 REPS DAILY
🪞 Mirror Posture Check
Stand barefoot facing a mirror, feet together. Check if your hips are level. Consciously stand with weight equally on both feet. Do this morning and evening.
2 MIN — MORNING & EVENING

⚠️ No heavy dumbbells or cable machine until waist is balanced. Start with 5 kg once corrected.  |  🗓️ Sunday: Full rest — sleep, hydrate, 10 min waist stretch only.  |  🏥 If you feel back or hip pain, stop and consult a physiotherapist.
